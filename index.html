Chromatic Ascension

Original digital artwork, made by hand by Eli Kenny.

Chromatic Ascension is the art practice of Eli Kenny — original digital pieces built up by hand, not generated. Work is sold as digital art packs: wallpapers, desktop sets and visuals for streaming.

About this site

This page is the home of the Chromatic Ascension art practice and the reference site for Chromatic Ascension Listing Manager, a private tool the artist uses to manage listings for his own shop. The tool creates and updates listings, uploads listing images, and sets inventory and pricing. It is used by the shop owner only and is not distributed.
